Psychology is the scientific study of the mind and behavior, encompassing conscious and unconscious  phenomena, including thoughts, emotions, and motives. It uses empirical methods to describe,  explain, predict, and change behavior, with goals to improve me ntal health and daily life. Major  branches include clinical, cognitive, behavioral, developmental, and social psychology.  Key Aspects of Psychology  Definition & Goal: It is the scientific study of human (and nonhuman) behavior and mental processes.  Psychologists aim to understand how the brain functions, how we develop, and how we interact with  our environment.  Scientific Method: Psychologists use rigorous research methods — such as experiments, observations,  and questionnaires — to test hypotheses and develop theories.  Core Pillars: The discipline bridges natural and social sciences, focusing on biological, cognitive, and  social perspectives.
